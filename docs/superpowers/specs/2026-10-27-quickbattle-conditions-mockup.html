Two equal columns split by a rule. The fixed defaults are dashed and quiet, with no ×. Added conditions are solid cards with one × top right. ANY / ALL reads as a sentence. Ships are the setup screen's own rows, each with its art (the coloured edge is its group's allegiance) and its own ×.

"+ Add condition" opens the setup screen's usual drop menu: Ships destroyed, Timer. "+ Add ship" opens the same menu listing the ships this side may name (enemies for Victory; friendly and neutral for Defeat).
